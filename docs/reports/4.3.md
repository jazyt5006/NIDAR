# 4.3: Venue grid registration: uncertainty, wall-alignment estimator, preflight

Evidence level of every result: unit / simulation (synthetic mazes and scenarios; nothing hardware or recorded).

## Summary
- PASS: R1, R2, R3, R4.
- Registration uncertainty (origin, yaw) is propagated with Jacobians to the fused track position at the cell-probability step only; a wall-alignment estimator (OFF by default, assumes walls on grid lines, O1/O9) refines the registration and feeds the Layer 5 update path with hysteresis; `python -m survivor_tagging.preflight` lists every unset parameter as blocking or non-blocking.
- Details and full tables: `docs/reports/4.3_eval.txt` (5 seeds, run time 181 s).

## Ambiguities and how they were handled
- Jacobian sign: the task's dg/dtheta = R'(theta)(p-o) depends on convention; implemented for g = S R(-yaw)(p-o) and verified by finite differences.
- Origin and yaw errors are treated as independent; registration estimator uncertainty is bootstrap (statistical) only.
- "Without propagation" in R2 = sigma 0.0. Registration of the scenarios' pipeline is configured wrong and never corrected.
- Walls are assumed to give points on the wall centre line; thickness bias is not modelled.
