## Ambiguities and how they were handled
- **Rule as written.** Only pairs that gate with a common track are dropped. A tight track can leave a second box 0.4 m away
  outside its gate; that box starts a track and the pair merge below consolidates the two. I tried "one box gates, the other
  none = duplicate", but it collapses real tracks 0.3 m apart (existing `test_observation_between_two_tracks_is_ambiguous`).
- **Keep, not fuse:** both boxes come from the same depth image; fusing would be overconfident.
- **Pair merge:** `_merge` may merge two tracks that share frames if in every shared frame the two observations are < 0.5 m
  apart, the tracks are < 0.5 m apart and they pass the usual chi-square merge gate. Each shared frame's duplicate observation
  is dropped and the streak is reset. Merging runs before the proximity flag, so a pair of one body leaves no "too close" warning.
- **Streak recovery:** an unambiguous assignment resets the streak of every track the observation gates with.
- **Scenario placement:** double_detection keeps the person's centre at x 3.35-4.0, y 3.1-3.9 of cell (3,3). With the whole cell,
  2 of 5 seeds at offset 0.0 landed in cell (2,3) through the front-surface bias (as in `standing`), which is not duplicate
  handling. This is a scenario design choice.
- **0.1-0.4 m apart:** a flat-faced body has one surface point. A box only partly on the body lands on the wall behind it
  (estimates 2.4-3 m apart: a wrong surface, not a duplicate). So the second box is the first widened sideways by 0.1-0.4 m,
  and the two ESTIMATES differ by 0.00-0.05 m (tested: median < 0.1 m, 90th percentile < 0.25 m). Pairs 0.1-0.4 m apart
  are covered at the Layer 4 unit level only.
- **Simulator drift:** live poses before the first closure keep the scripted drift without covariance (existing tests pin them).
  After a closure the drift rate is random per segment, so the fixed +y direction of repeated_corrections and
  frame_correction is gone.
